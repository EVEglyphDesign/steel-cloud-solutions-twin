Why SAP benefits from an actor like us on the client side

Additive to the SI. Client-side by design. Zero responsibility for the SI's scope.

SIs do exceptional SAP delivery work — configuration, integration, testing, cutover. That is their scope and it is not ours. What we do is the work outside that scope: the client-side fit-to-standard readiness, the DataSphere stand-up, the client's own AI reasoning on top of SAP-governed data. Our posture is straightforward: anything that is not SAP's product, the client claims for themselves — while still respecting SAP's semantic layer and SAP's AI consumption models. Nothing gets taken from anyone's plate. That alignment is what SAP wants and what the client needs, and it is what our client-side, non-channel-partner posture is built to deliver.

The AI protocol problem, plainly

Generative tools produce documentation faster than any team can review it.

When every contributor points a different tool at a different slice of the same SAP object, the outputs diverge instead of converging — and the review burden lands weeks later on the people least able to reconstruct what the tool actually did. A shared reference model and a small set of control documents fix this at the source, not after the fact.

01 · DICOE

SCS Decision Intelligence Centre of Excellence

DataSphere stand-up, done fast, run by your own people afterwards.

The problem this solves

AI vendors are trying to access SAP data without respecting the semantic layer. When a client is under that pressure — and especially when they are already on S/4 without DataSphere and do not believe they can afford it — the alternative path being sold to them is replication out to a third-party platform. If those replication flows go into production before SAP's own analytics stack is stood up, the workload leaves the SAP compute envelope for a long time. Potentially permanently.

DataSphere is the door to SAC — SAP Analytics Cloud, which is the analytics product SAP's Databricks partnership is built on — and to Joule, SAP's generative AI copilot, which is already embedded inside DataSphere and SAC today. If DataSphere is not up, that entire stack is not up either.

What we do

We stand DataSphere up on your own tenant, using your own SAP-connection patterns, and we constitute the history — populating DataSphere with your SAP data history so the analytics are real from day one, not a demo tenant with placeholder data. We do this in a small unit of work rather than a long programme. We train a named person on your side to run the platform after we leave — not a slide deck, a working operator who can defend the platform in their own room.

How we unlock the licensing economically

SAP offers a demonstration licensing window for DataSphere. When we know the type of work the client wants done ahead of time, our tools let us set the environment up quickly and economically inside that window — so the client sees the analytics working on their own history before the commercial licensing conversation begins. The client evaluates a live thing on their own data, not a slide.

Who is actually on the keyboard

US-fronted delivery, backed by an SAP Gold Partner Basis team. The hands touching your tenant are among the highest SAP-certified for technical proficiency — a bench most client-side teams cannot assemble on their own and most SIs will not spare for a stand-up unit. Client-facing conversations, decisions and hand-back are run by native-English, US-based resources; the deep specialist work is done by the certified team the US resources front for. You get both, at the cost of a small unit of work.

The training took months and the learning was real. What comes back to the shelf now is that learning, turned into a repeatable small unit.

02 · EVE DI PMO

Structured Repository & Human-in-the-Loop protocols

The base-model install and education service for clients getting organised for an AI-augmented initiative.

What the SI channel is not in a position to provide

Getting an enterprise IT team ready to work with AI on an SAP programme is a specific piece of work that the incumbent SI is usually not set up to deliver. It is not implementation, it is not training in the classical sense, and it does not belong inside the SI statement of work. It belongs on the client side, before the AI-augmented work starts, so that when it does start it does not fragment.

Two things get installed inside your tenant

A structured repository, seeded with your own SAP shape, indexed so every query gets cheaper and more accurate over time rather than more expensive and more speculative. Repository indices and READMEs behind each surface are refined as the surfaces are used — the data itself becomes more valuable each week it sits in place.

A Human-in-the-Loop protocol layer: a small set of control documents that individual contributors — human and AI — work to. The protocol is what makes automated documentation reviewable; without it, the review burden is unbounded and the project silently falls apart.

How this connects to SAP Activate

We layer this on top of SAP Activate. We leverage Activate, we do not replace it. What we add is a numbering-based reference model that traces every technical object back through its functional specification and back through its source artifact. That trace is what makes AI review meaningful: the AI has something structured to reason over, and a human reviewer has something to check the AI against.

Training is included at low cost

Methodology training — your staff, working to the reference model and the control documents, so the project stays coherent as generative tooling is layered in.

SAP AI tool training — your staff, using SAP's own AI tooling correctly, so the CoE outputs stay compatible with the standard.

Deliverables
Structured repository stood up on your tenant · numbering-based reference model seeded with your SAP shape · HITL control documents · two training tracks · a review pattern your architects can actually run.
