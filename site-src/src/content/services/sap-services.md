## Where this fits

Most SAP engagements fail on the same axis: the client pays for capacity while the value is locked inside a small number of senior heads. We invert that. The senior heads produce a working repository of your program in the first week, and the delivery capacity scales against that repository — cheaply, correctly, and on demand.

## What we bring that a standard SI does not

- **A sovereign toolchain** built around Claude and Perplexity, tuned against a private repository of SAP patterns, blueprints, and configuration precedents we have accumulated across two decades of engagements. Your program benefits from every prior engagement without leaking any of your data into anyone else's.
- **Advisory-grade solution architects and functional consultants** whose careers were built on the client side of SAP implementations. The people writing your blueprint have lived on the receiving end of one.
- **A development center in Hyderabad** operating under a multi-year gold-partner relationship. The team is known to us. The escalation path is direct. Resource risk is materially lower than any spot-market SI can offer.
- **Fixed-price, deliverable-scoped commercial terms** on the units of work that lend themselves to it. No parking of consultants on your budget.

## Who this is for

- **SAP account teams** who need pre-sales and demonstration support they can bring into a customer conversation this week.
- **System integrators** who need a specialist bench to fill a specific gap — an integration, a blueprint refresh, a technical spec, a configuration piece — without giving up the account.
- **SAP customers directly** who need the human side of the change managed by people whose heritage is in their seat, not the vendor's.

## How to start

Every engagement starts with a mirror and a working session. The mirror is free to set up on the smaller programs; the working session is a fixed-fee scoping conversation that produces a written engagement brief on the same day. From there, we agree deliverables, price, and dates.
