CPQ Migration

Migrate CPQ without breaking sales.

Salesforce CPQ migrations — from legacy CPQ to Revenue Cloud, or between CPQ platforms — done in a way that keeps the sales team quoting on Monday morning. Our Salesforce channel-partner practice has been doing this shape of work for two decades.

Why CPQ migrations fail

Every CPQ implementation carries a decade of accumulated pricing logic, approval rules, discount thresholds, product bundles, and territory carve-outs. Most of it lives in the heads of two or three people in the RevOps team. A migration project that treats CPQ as a schema-to-schema data move — instead of a controlled port of the pricing logic — will ship on time, break every deal in the pipeline the following week, and be reversed inside a quarter.

We treat CPQ migration as an assessment problem first and an implementation problem second. Before we touch the target platform, we produce an inventory of every price rule, every approval path, every discount tier, and every product bundle the source system carries — with the business rationale for each one attached, drawn from your RevOps team's actual knowledge and the historical quote data.

How we scope

  • Discovery. Two to four weeks. We build the inventory. Deliverable is a document your RevOps team, your finance team, and your sales operations team all sign against.
  • Design. Three to six weeks. Target-state configuration on the destination CPQ, with every source rule accounted for — mapped, deprecated, or explicitly parked.
  • Cutover. One to three months, depending on the complexity of the pipeline. Parallel run on live quotes with reconciliation reporting until the sales team is confident.
  • Hyper-care. Four to eight weeks of on-call support after cutover. Not a retainer; a fixed-window commitment.

What we deliver alongside

  • An accurate pricing-rule inventory that survives the migration — useful for the next reorganization, the next acquisition, or the next audit.
  • Sales-team enablement material written for the actual quoting flow after cutover.
  • A go/no-go framework for every quote in flight during the cutover window, so nothing gets stuck.

Where we fit

We take direct CPQ migration engagements, and we also partner with SIs and with Salesforce itself on programmes where a specialist CPQ team is needed for a specific workstream. Both patterns are common. If you already have an SI running the broader Salesforce programme, we can plug in on the CPQ-specific work without disturbing the wider engagement.
