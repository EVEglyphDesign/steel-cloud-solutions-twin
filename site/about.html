About SCS

A small shop that keeps things running.

Steel Cloud Solutions is a boutique enterprise-software consultancy, based in Kansas City. We are an official Salesforce channel partner and a client-side SAP advisor. Our practice is intentionally small — that is what lets us bring senior specialists to a client's table on the specific question that actually needs them.

What we are

Two practices under one roof. The Salesforce practice is a mature, logo'd channel partnership — CPQ, custom portals, complex integrations, and the migrations that come with them. It is where Steel Cloud Solutions was built and it remains the majority of our recurring work.

The SAP practice is client-side advisory: training, integration evaluation, project planning, and specialist technical staffing for enterprise IT teams running or evaluating SAP. We are not an SAP channel partner and we do not resell SAP licences. We work alongside your existing SI, with SAP itself, and with the other vendors on your list.

What we are not

We are not a big-four consultancy. We are not a system integrator. We are not a reseller. We do not sell an AI product and we are not building one. When we bring modern tooling to a client engagement — and we do, frequently — it runs on infrastructure the client already owns, and the client keeps custody.

Where we sit in the ecosystem

Additive, by design. Most of our engagements are structured so the client's existing partners keep their scope, and we come in on a specific question that would otherwise stall the programme. In practice, that means we frequently work with or via an SI rather than in place of one. Direct client engagements happen, but they are not the default entry point and they are not required for us to be useful.

The people

Our practice is anchored by senior subject-matter experts — SAP and Salesforce practitioners with the kind of heritage that lets a client trust the answer. We pair that expertise with a professional community and a flexible labor pool that lets us staff specialist work at the right time-window and the right rate, rather than as a full-time hire the client will regret in six months.

How to reach us

Most conversations start with a short scoping call. There is no standard pitch deck and no obligation. If we are the wrong fit for the work you are describing, we say so, and where possible we point you at someone who is the right fit.

Email us at contact@steelcloudsolutions.com, or reach out via LinkedIn. We aim to respond within one business day.
