02 · EVE DI PMO

Structured Repository & Human-in-the-Loop protocols

The base-model install and education service for clients getting organised for an AI-augmented initiative.

What the SI channel is not in a position to provide

Getting an enterprise IT team ready to work with AI on an SAP programme is a specific piece of work that the incumbent SI is usually not set up to deliver. It is not implementation, it is not training in the classical sense, and it does not belong inside the SI statement of work. It belongs on the client side, before the AI-augmented work starts, so that when it does start it does not fragment.

Two things get installed inside your tenant

A structured repository, seeded with your own SAP shape, indexed so every query gets cheaper and more accurate over time rather than more expensive and more speculative. Repository indices and READMEs behind each surface are refined as the surfaces are used — the data itself becomes more valuable each week it sits in place.

A Human-in-the-Loop protocol layer: a small set of control documents that individual contributors — human and AI — work to. The protocol is what makes automated documentation reviewable; without it, the review burden is unbounded and the project silently falls apart.

How this connects to SAP Activate

We layer this on top of SAP Activate. We leverage Activate, we do not replace it. What we add is a numbering-based reference model that traces every technical object back through its functional specification and back through its source artifact. That trace is what makes AI review meaningful: the AI has something structured to reason over, and a human reviewer has something to check the AI against.

Training is included at low cost

Methodology training — your staff, working to the reference model and the control documents, so the project stays coherent as generative tooling is layered in.

SAP AI tool training — your staff, using SAP's own AI tooling correctly, so the CoE outputs stay compatible with the standard.

Deliverables
Structured repository stood up on your tenant · numbering-based reference model seeded with your SAP shape · HITL control documents · two training tracks · a review pattern your architects can actually run.

SAP competitors are quick to overstate the difficulty of respecting SAP's semantic layer while doing modern reasoning over enterprise data. Any AI product that walks into the room from that point forward — SAP's, a hyperscaler's, or a boutique's — is evaluated against the same governed baseline.
