SAP practice

Client-side SAP advisory. Additive, not competing.

We are not an SAP channel partner and we do not resell SAP licences. We support enterprise IT teams on the client side of an SAP engagement — training, integration evaluation, project planning, and specialist technical staffing. That means we work well with your existing SI, with SAP directly, and with the vendors already on your list.

01

Client-side training

Change management and adoption for the people who will actually use the SAP surface after go-live. We prepare your business users and your IT team to run the platform without needing the SI on speed dial.

02

Integration evaluation

A structured read of the SAP-adjacent landscape you already run — data volumes, hand-off points, exception patterns. What to trust, what to fix, what to sunset. Delivered as a document your architects can use, not a sales deck.

03

Project planning

Data-grounded discovery for SAP transformation work. We use modern tools to process the data your organization already has, so decisions get made on the actual dollars, cents, and transaction volumes instead of on hopeful assumptions.

04

Specialist technical staffing

Hard-to-find SAP expertise for the length of time it takes to do the work — not a full-time hire for an eight-week problem. A flexible labor pool of known-quantity practitioners, drawn from a professional community we've built over decades.

How we engage

The consultative approach, in three moves.

01

We listen

Scope conversation with your team and your existing partners. No standard deck. We map the actual question before proposing anything.

02

We fit in

Our work sits alongside what you already have. Working with or via an existing SI is the default. No cannibalization of vendor relationships.

03

We hand it back

Every deliverable is written to be usable without us. Your team keeps custody. Nothing we install requires our continued presence.

04

We stay only if useful

Ongoing advisory retainers exist, but they are opt-in. If the work is done, the engagement closes. Additive by design.
