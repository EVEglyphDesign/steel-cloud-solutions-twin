SAP client-side technical services.

Four things we do for SAP clients — DataSphere stand-up and ongoing support, recorded fit-to-standard sessions run on-site, semantic access to SAP, and setting up SAP products. Delivered by older, more experienced consultants with a lot of on-site experience. Additive to your SI. Accelerator to your SAP Activate schedule.

01

DataSphere stand-up, BootCamp handover, ongoing support

End-to-end DataSphere tenant stand-up, BootCamp-style handover to your team, and ongoing support on backlog requests as they come in. This is the work we like most and the work we keep going consistently.

Who this is for

SAP account-executive teams that have DataSphere-related solution-design backlog they cannot staff internally, and SAP clients that need a DataSphere tenant stood up cleanly the first time.

How we run it

Short stand-up window. Artifact-first. Everything committed to the client's repository. BootCamp handover trains the client's own people to run it after we leave. Ongoing support runs on request — no retainer required.

Why it works

We have done this before, we do it consistently, and the same senior technical bench keeps doing it — which is how the stand-up finishes on schedule and the ongoing support stays cheap for you to consume.

02

Recorded fit-to-standard assessment sessions, run on-site

Structured on-site sessions where we sit in the room with your team, use the AI tools and infrastructure you already own, and produce a recorded fit-to-standard assessment of your business against SAP standard. The recording is the artifact — in your custody from the moment it exists.

Why on-site matters

The sessions run faster and produce better decisions when we are in the room. We are older, more experienced consultants with a lot of on-site delivery experience — that experience is why we can extract the answer in a session instead of dragging it out through a workshop series.

How we use your AI tools

Your enterprise IT has already licensed AI tools. We show your team how to use those tools, on your own infrastructure, to accelerate the fit-to-standard work — pulling together the information you need, faster, without adding a new AI license to your procurement pile.

What you get

A recorded set of sessions, structured to be re-viewable by anyone in the organisation who needs the context later. Notes and decisions committed to the client's repository. A working understanding of where you fit SAP standard and where you do not, sharp enough to negotiate scope with your SI.

03

Semantic access to SAP

Setting up your client-side access to the SAP semantic layer so your teams and your tools consume SAP data correctly — not through raw table dumps or replicated flows that lose the semantics on the way out.

Why it matters

Most SAP clients in the mid-market are consuming SAP data through routes that strip the semantic layer. Downstream analytics, AI, and reporting silently degrade. Once semantic access is set up correctly, everything downstream improves without changing anything else.

What we deliver

The connection, the documentation of how it is set up, and the training your team needs to use it — again, committed to your repository so nothing depends on us being in the room afterwards.

04

Setting up SAP products

SAP-product installation, configuration, and tenant stand-up. General technical bench work on SAP-side tooling, delivered as small units of work rather than long engagements.

Who is on the keyboard

US-fronted delivery, backed by an SAP Gold Partner Basis team. Client-facing conversations, decisions, and hand-back are run by US-based consultants with strong communication skills, deep industry knowledge, and cultural-awareness training. The deep specialist work is done by the certified team the US resources front for. You get both, at the cost of a small unit of work.

How we run it

Small unit-of-work engagements, delivered as promised, with the setup documented in your repository so your team can run it after we leave.

How we deliver

On-site when it matters. US-fronted. Artifact-first. Everything in your repository.
