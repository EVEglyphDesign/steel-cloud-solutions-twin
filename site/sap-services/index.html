How it works

The Claude-driven engagement model

01

Project mirror

Every engagement starts by mirroring your existing project artifacts — SOWs, blueprints, specs, configuration workbooks — into a private, versioned repository we operate for you. The mirror is the ground truth. Nothing we produce depends on chat history.

02

Automated production

From the mirror, our tools produce blueprint designs, functional and technical specifications, framework designs, and wireframes automatically. You review, we iterate. What used to take a bench of consultants two weeks lands in days.

03

Advisory or training — often both

The same engagement can be delivered as a consulting event or as a training event for your team. Our default posture is both: we do the work in the open and your people learn the method as it happens.

04

Hyderabad development center

When configuration or development is needed, our gold-partner delivery center in Hyderabad — the same people we have worked with for years — scales in piece-by-piece. Treat them as our development center, because for you they are.
