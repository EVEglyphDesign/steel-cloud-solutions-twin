SAP Services

Client-side SAP advisory. Additive, not competing.

We are not an SAP channel partner and we do not resell SAP licences. We work on the client side of an SAP engagement — for the enterprise IT team that has to live with the system after go-live, and for the SIs and SAP itself when specialist advisory is what would move a programme forward.

The four service categories

What we do, in plain terms.

01 · Training

Client-side training and adoption

Change management and adoption for the people who will actually use the SAP surface after go-live. We prepare your business users and your IT team to run the platform without needing the SI on speed dial. A boutique adoption specialist, focused on AI safety, data governance, and treating data as a capital asset.

02 · Evaluation

Integration evaluation

A structured read of the SAP-adjacent landscape you already run — data volumes, hand-off points, exception patterns, the places where the system knows something and the business does not. Delivered as a document your architects can use, not a sales deck.

03 · Planning

Project planning and discovery

Data-grounded discovery for SAP transformation work. We process the data your organization already has, so the requirements conversation gets grounded in actual dollars, cents, and transaction volumes — not in hopeful assumptions. The client makes decisions faster and both sides waste less.

04 · Staffing

Specialist technical staffing

Hard-to-find SAP expertise for the length of time it takes to do the work — not a full-time hire for an eight-week problem. A flexible pool of known-quantity practitioners drawn from a professional community built over decades. Right-sized time windows, no long-term overhead.

Positioning

Additive by design.

Most enterprise clients already have an SI, an incumbent BI vendor, a hyperscaler, and one or two specialist consultancies on retainer. We are not looking to replace any of them. Our engagements are additive — the client gets a capability they did not have, without giving up a relationship they already trust.

In practice this means we frequently work with or via an existing SI. We can also take direct referrals, but that is not the default entry point and it is not required. We do not compete on the account, and we are explicit about that in every scoping conversation.

What we bring to the table is a combination that is hard to source in one place: senior subject-matter experts — practitioners with the kind of SAP heritage that makes clients trust the answer — paired with modern tooling that lets the assessment work move at a different speed. The client keeps full custody of everything we build. No new AI product to license. No Kool-Aid to drink. Nothing that requires our continued presence to keep running.

Why this shape

The economy we can actually offer.

Enterprise SAP work has a specific staffing problem: the questions that come up during a programme are frequently narrow and time-boxed. A supply-chain question during a design review. A data-quality question during a reconciliation. A user-adoption question in the last month before cutover. These are exactly the kind of questions where hiring a full-time expert costs six months of salary to answer a two-week question, and where relying on the SI's junior bench produces an answer nobody trusts.

Our practice is built around this gap. We can bring a senior specialist to the table for the time it takes them to figure out the answer, and if there is downstream execution work, we can staff that separately at a different rate. The client pays for the expertise where the expertise is needed, not on a blended-rate contract that dilutes both.

The AI angle, without the pitch

We use modern AI tooling to accelerate assessment work — ingesting a client's own documentation, contracts, exception logs, and data extracts so that the senior SME arrives at the conversation already up to speed. This means the specialist spends their time on the reasoning that requires human judgement, not on the reading that a well-designed pipeline can do overnight.

Two things matter about how we do this:

  • The tooling runs on infrastructure the client already owns. No new AI product to license, no data leaving the client boundary, no vendor lock-in.
  • The tooling is transparent. Every AI-generated summary is traceable to the source document. Every derived number is traceable to the SAP extract it came from. Nothing is a black box.

When we are the right fit

  • You are early in an SAP transformation and the requirements conversation is not converging.
  • You are in the middle of an implementation and a specific workstream is stuck on a question your SI cannot answer at the depth you need.
  • You are post-go-live and the adoption curve is flatter than the business case assumed.
  • You are an SI or SAP itself, and you need specialist advisory or staffing to move a client engagement forward.

When we are the wrong fit

  • You are looking to buy an AI product. We do not sell one.
  • You are looking to replace your SI. We work alongside them, not around them.
  • You are looking for the cheapest hourly rate. Specialist advisory is priced against the value of the answer, not against a commodity bench.
